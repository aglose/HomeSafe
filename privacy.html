Privacy policy

HomeSafe is a private app used only by the members of one household, on a server the household owns. This page says what it does with Google account data.

Gmail

  • A household member may let HomeSafe read their Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly), so that their household budget can show what Amazon orders contained.
  • It searches only for Amazon's order and shipment emails. From those it keeps the order number, date, item titles, quantities, prices and totals. It does not keep the emails, and it never reads other mail, sends mail, or changes anything in the mailbox.
  • What it keeps stays on the household's own server. It is shown only to that household's members, in their own app, and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. Item titles are sorted by a model that runs on the same server, so they don't leave it.
  • HomeSafe's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Asking the household's administrator deletes what was kept.
